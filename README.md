# DMart-Sales-Operational-Intelligence-Dashboard

Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze D-Mart retail sales performance, customer demographics, product contribution, purchasing behavior, and operational metrics.
The dashboard helps transform sales data into meaningful business insights through KPI cards, charts, tables, and interactive filters.

Problem Statement
D-Mart, a leading retail chain, wants to upgrade its decision-making with data-driven insights. The goal is to:
● Analyze customer purchase patterns.
● Understand sales trends and product performance.
● Explore operational metrics such as shipping and payment status.
● Build a Power BI dashboard to drive strategic business decisions.

Project Objectives
Analyze total revenue, order volume, average order value, and cancellations.
Compare sales trends across years and months.
Understand customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
Identify products and product types contributing to revenue.
Analyze payment methods and shipping modes.
Compare discount bands and product ratings.
Monitor state-level sales and cancellation metrics.

Dataset
The dashboard analyzes retail data related to:

Key Findings
Overall Sales Performance: The dashboard reports $17.87 million in revenue, 25,000 orders, an average order value of $775.36, and 1,784 cancellations.
Sales Trends: Monthly sales fluctuate across 2021–2023, indicating potential seasonal patterns that can support sales forecasting and inventory planning.
Product Performance: Local products contribute the highest revenue ($8.94 million), followed by branded products ($5.41 million) and imported products ($3.53 million).
Customer Demographics: Male customers contribute approximately 57% of revenue, compared with 43% from female customers, while average order values remain similar.
Payment and Shipping: Debit appears to be the leading payment category, while free shipping accounts for the largest share of orders.
Discount Analysis: The moderate discount band (10–25%) generates the highest revenue, at approximately $6.68 million.
Operational Performance: Maharashtra records the highest revenue among the four displayed states, while Telangana has the lowest displayed cancellation metric.
Customer Insights: The dashboard suggests no clear relationship between website engagement, discount levels, and customer purchasing behavior or product ratings.

Business Recommendations
Focus on high-performing products, investigate cancellation patterns, evaluate shipping costs, optimize discount strategies, and monitor customer satisfaction to support data-driven business decisions.
